cBioPortal for Cancer Genomics

Make complex cancer genomic data accessible and interpretable
Open source and open data, maintained by MSK, Dana-Farber, UHN, CHOP, The Hyve and SE4BIO
For those less familiar: cohort exploration, gene-centric queries like OncoPrints and lollipops, group comparison, and patient timelines, across 550+ curated studies. Open source, multi-institution.

cBioChat: Everyday Questions

Query TCGA and look up treatments in MSK-CHORD, each confirmed in cBioPortal

cBioPortal in Your Own AI

Add the MCP connector once, then ask from the assistant you already use
mcp.cbioportal.org/db/mcp · mcp.cbioportal.org/navigator/mcp · illustration with real query results

Most common failure
Asking a clarifying question instead of acting
github.com/cBioPortal/cbioportal-mcp-qa
Four tracks: data (62), analysis (46), navigation (26), out of scope (12). Questions are asked to the deployed agent; the judge sees the tool calls, so computed statistics aren't marked as invented. Haiku 5.5 gets two-thirds right for under a cent. Run-to-run noise is large (Sonnet 5: 62–75% across runs). Each failure becomes a guide change and stays as a regression test.
